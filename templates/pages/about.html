The idea behind EcoBin

A cleaner routine for the places existing services miss.

EcoBin Ghana is a proposed subscription-based waste collection and recycling rewards platform starting in Greater Accra.

A focused launch

Reliability at the doorstep.
Recycling in the system.

EcoBin connects households, schools, restaurants and small businesses with contracted pickup agents through one digital service.

The model is intentionally asset-light: local collectors handle routes while the platform coordinates bookings, status updates, support and the rewards wallet.
