Questions, answered

Know what happens before you book.

A clear service is easier to trust. Here are the current answers based on the EcoBin launch model.

Where does EcoBin operate?

The launch focus is Greater Accra, serving urban and peri-urban customers. The business plan identifies wider expansion as a future possibility, not a current service promise.

Who is EcoBin for?

EcoBin is designed for households and estates, schools, restaurants and small businesses that need more dependable collection and a practical recycling routine.

How do the rewards work?

Recyclable material is recorded by a collector and must be verified before points are added. Available points can be redeemed for catalogue rewards such as airtime, vouchers and bill discounts.

What are the current plans?

Basic is GHS 40 per month, Standard is GHS 75 per month, and Business uses custom pricing for daily collection and operational support.

Is payment live?

The application is prepared for server-side Paystack verification. Live checkout should only be enabled after the Paystack credentials and account configuration are supplied.

What happens if material is not approved?

A recycling record can be rejected during review. Rejected or pending records do not issue reward points.
